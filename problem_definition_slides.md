# UOkay?
### University Health Center AI Frontline Triage & Clinician Workstation

**Product Overview**
*Safe, Closed-Loop Clinical Intake for Campus Healthcare*

---

## 1. The Problem: Bottlenecks & Risky AI

* **Intake Bottleneck:** Providers lose **10–15 minutes** per visit asking basic questions (symptom onset, duration, allergies).
* **Disconnected EHR History:** Past lab results and chronic records are isolated from the initial student check-in.
* **The AI Liability Trap:** Generic chatbots hallucinate diagnoses, suggest medications, or miss life-threatening emergencies.

---

## 2. The Solution: Safe Intake + Command Center

* **Context-Aware Intake:** Gathers symptom details while connecting directly to mock EHR records (e.g., past ferritin, spirometry, HbA1c).
* **Strict Medical Boundaries:** Never prescribes, diagnoses, or excuses classes; human clinicians retain 100% of medical authority.
* **Instant Red-Flag Escalation:** Immediately halts routine intake and triggers **911 / Campus EMS (ext. 3131)** for acute symptoms.

---

## 3. Closed-Loop Care: From Intake to Action

* **Provider Command Center:** Clinicians review structured queues, schedule in-person visits, and order tailored diagnostic labs.
* **Real-Time Student Notices:** Confirmed bookings and lab orders instantly post into the student's conversation stream.
* **AI Care Plan Awareness:** Gemini is aware of provider orders and accurately answers student questions about upcoming appointments.

---

## 4. Live Platform & Key Takeaway

* **5 Distinct Patient Profiles:** Tested across diverse clinical cases (anemia, asthma, diabetes, migraines, mono).
* **Flexible Workstation:** Seamless single-pane intake chat, full clinician queue, or dual view.
* **100% Human-in-the-Loop:** Dramatically speeds up clinic intake while completely protecting patient safety.

<div class="card">
  <strong>Bottom Line:</strong> Faster triage for students, zero paperwork burden for clinicians, and complete clinical safety.
